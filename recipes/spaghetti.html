This is a delicious and hearty spaghetti recipe.

Ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 3 cloves garlic

Steps

  1. Cook the spaghetti according to package instructions in salted boiling water. Drain, but reserve ½ cup of pasta water.
  2. In a pan, melt butter over medium heat. Add garlic and chili flakes, sauté for 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
  3. Add the drained spaghetti and mix well, adding a little pasta water to loosen the sauce and enjoy!
