Lasagna

Home

Description

This is a delicious recipe and hearty lasagna recipe.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 jar of marinara
  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions.
  3. Layer the ingredients in a baking dish and bake for 45 minutes.
