Description

This is a delicious and hearty salad recipe.

Ingredients

  • 1 cucumber (diced)
  • 2 tomatoes (chopped)
  • 1/2 red onion
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

    In a bowl, combine cucumber, tomatoes, red onion, feta, and olives.
    Drizzle with olive oil and lemon juice.
    Season with salt and pepper, then toss well and enjoy!
